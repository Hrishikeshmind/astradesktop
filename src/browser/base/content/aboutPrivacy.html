Astra Browser

Privacy Notice

This notice ships with the browser so it stays available offline. Astra is an independent Firefox-based browser. Your browsing data lives in your local profile on this device.

What stays on your computer

  • History, cookies, passwords, and extensions in your profile folder
  • Settings you choose in Preferences, including privacy presets
  • Session restore data if you leave that option enabled

What Astra does not run as a cloud account

Astra has no required sign-in. Features such as Search Hub and the AI sidebar only talk to a site or provider when you use them. Queries and chats then follow that provider’s own terms.

Firefox platform services

Astra inherits Firefox platform components (certificate checking, Safe Browsing lists, captive-portal detection, and similar). You can review related controls under Settings → Privacy and security.
