Astra Browser

Translate pages

Astra can translate web pages you are reading into a language you choose. Translation runs on your computer — the page text is not sent to a cloud translation service for processing.

How to use it

  • Open a page in a language you want translated.
  • Use the translate icon in the address bar, the app menu, or the right-click menu.
  • Pick the language you want and confirm.

Privacy

On-device translation keeps the page content local to your machine. You stay in control: nothing is translated until you ask for it.

Limits

Some pages or languages may not be supported yet. If translation is unavailable, Astra will tell you in the panel instead of failing silently.
