Privacy Features Changelog

Astra Browser

Switching to a new device

Astra does not yet offer a full cross-device sync service or mobile app like some other browsers. This page explains what you can do today and what is still planned.

What works today

  • Your bookmarks, history, passwords, and settings live in your local profile on this computer.
  • Scheduled profile backup (when enabled) saves a copy you can restore manually under Settings.
  • Firefox Account sign-in remains available for platform features that still use Mozilla’s sync infrastructure, but Astra does not ship a dedicated mobile companion yet.

Moving to another computer

Until guided sync is a first-class Astra feature, move data by restoring from a backup or exporting bookmarks and passwords through Settings. Do not expect automatic real-time sync across devices yet.

What is coming

Cross-device sync and clearer account defaults are on the backlog. Track progress on GitHub.
